Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026.

What we collect

Your email and account data (for login and payouts), your coin ledger (offers completed, withdrawals), device fingerprint and IP (fraud prevention), and usage data. We do not collect payment card details — PayPal and crypto payouts are handled by those providers.

Cookies & third parties

We use cookies for login sessions and preferences. Third parties on this site include: our backend (Supabase), offerwall partners (Offerwall.GG, AdGem), survey routers (CPX Research, TheoremReach), and display ad networks (Infolinks, Monetag, Adsterra, Adcash, HilltopAds, Clickadu, RichAds, Evadav, Mondiad, Media.net). These third parties may set cookies (including ad cookies) and collect device/IP data under their own privacy policies. You can opt out of interest-based ads via your browser settings and the opt-out links in each network's policy.

How we use data

To run accounts, credit coins, process withdrawals, prevent fraud, and improve the site. We do not sell your personal data.

Your rights

You can request a copy or deletion of your account data by emailing support. US state privacy laws (including California's CCPA/CPRA) give you rights to know, delete, and opt out of sale/sharing — we honor those requests even though we are below the statutory thresholds. We do not serve users under 13.
