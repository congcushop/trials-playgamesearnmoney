About TrialEarn

TrialEarn is a get-paid-to (GPT) site: advertisers pay us when you try their free trial, answer their survey, or play their game — and you keep 60% of that money as coins. 1,000 coins = $1. Cash out from $10 PayPal or $3 crypto.

We started TrialEarn because most GPT sites either hide their rates, promise impossible earnings, or show fake payout screenshots. We do the opposite:

  • Honest rates — the coin value of every offer is shown before you start.
  • Real payout proof — our proof wall lists only withdrawals we actually paid.
  • No fake promises — you will not earn $500 in an hour here. Trials pay $1–$4, games up to $50+ over days of play. Realistic, steady earning.
  • Fair rules — one account per person, no bots, no VPNs, and first withdrawals are manually reviewed so fraudsters can't drain the payout pool.

TrialEarn is the trials-led site of a small family of GPT sites (games-led and surveys-led sister sites share the same accounts and balances). Questions? See our contact page.
